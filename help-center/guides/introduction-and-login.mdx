## Get Started

Welcome to your first week of Laminar Copilot! We're glad to partner with you and see how Copilot can help with your day-to-day AFP operations.

This entire Onboarding Module should get your Copilot workspace set up successfully so that you're ready to run your first schedule in Copilot on Friday.

---

## Log In

First step is to log in and access your account.

1. Navigate to **[app.laminarcopilot.com](http://app.laminarcopilot.com)**
2. **Log in** using the credentials provided in your Amazon Chime Room.
3. **Change your password** by clicking on the settings icon on the bottom left corner and clicking **My Profile**. *Keep your account secure!*

> **Tip:** We recommend using **Google Chrome** to access the site. It is accessible on all major browsers (Edge, Firefox, Safari), but works best in Chrome.
