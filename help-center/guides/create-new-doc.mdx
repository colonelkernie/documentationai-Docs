## Quick Guide

Make your first document in just a few clicks.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Workspace">
    Log into Laminar Copilot and select your workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'New Document'">
    Find the 'New Document' button at the top right and click it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Content">
    Give your document a title and fill in your content using the editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and Share">
    Click 'Save', then use the "Share" button to set permissions for teammates.
  </Step>
</Steps>
