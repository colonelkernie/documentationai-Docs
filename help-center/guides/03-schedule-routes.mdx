## Generate Your Schedule for the Week

Come Friday, Amazon will give you routes to schedule for the following week. Once you've accepted your routes, follow these steps to bring the route data into Copilot.

1. Go to **Amazon Relay** and **export your routes to a CSV**.
2. Go to [Copilot](https://app.laminarcopilot.com) and click **Import Routes**.
3. **Upload your CSV file** exported from Relay.
4. **Choose your scheduling method:**

   | Method | Description |
   |---|---|
   | **Run Dynamic Scheduler** | Copilot automatically assigns drivers and assets to all upcoming routes in the CSV based on driver preferences defined in the Driver Roster. |
   | **Import Schedules** | Imports all upcoming routes in the CSV into Copilot without automatically assigning drivers. You will need to manually assign drivers and assets to the imported routes. |
