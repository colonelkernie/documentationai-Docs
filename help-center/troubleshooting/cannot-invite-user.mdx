---
title: Cannot Invite Team Members
description: Resolve invite issues in Laminar Copilot.
---

## Issue

- Team invitation email not received
- User can't join workspace

## Possible Causes

- Wrong email address entered
- Spam filter blocking invite

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Double-check email">
    Ensure the email is typed correctly. Try sending the invite again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check spam folder">
    Ask the invitee to check their spam/junk folder for the email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact support">
    If still not working, email support@laminarcopilot.com with details.
  </Step>
</Steps>
