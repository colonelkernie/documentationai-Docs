Relay Integration syncs all driver assignments on your routes within Copilot directly to Amazon Relay. Once it's set up, you no longer need to manually select the driver assignment for each route in Relay — Copilot pushes your assignments over for you.

## Setting up Relay Integration

Relay Integration requires a one-time setup with our Support team, since it involves inviting Laminar Copilot to your Amazon Relay account with **Dispatcher access**.

<Callout kind="info">
  [Submit a support ticket](/submit-a-support-ticket) to get started. Our team will walk you through inviting Laminar Copilot to Relay with Dispatcher access.
</Callout>

## Syncing assignments to Relay

Once setup is complete, you can push your current driver assignments to Relay at any time:

1. From the Copilot dashboard, go to **Command Center** in the side navigation.

2. Open the **Schedule** tab.

3. Click **Sync to Relay** in the top right of the tab.

All driver assignments on your routes are sent to Amazon Relay, so you don't have to manually select them there.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Sync to Relay only pushes your assignments when you click it — it isn't automatic. If you reassign a driver on a route after syncing, click **Sync to Relay** again to send the updated assignment.
</Callout>
