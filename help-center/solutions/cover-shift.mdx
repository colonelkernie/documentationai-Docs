---
title: Cover Shift
description: >-
  Recruit drivers by text to fill an open shift when a route is unassigned
  or a driver rejects it.
---
This flow lets you fill an open shift — whether a driver rejected a route or the route was never assigned a driver — by texting eligible drivers and letting them claim it.

## Starting the flow

Click **Cover Shift** in the Edit Drawer of an upcoming route to start.

## The recruitment flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter reason for callout" icon="pencil" titleType="h3">
    Only shown when recruiting for a route that isn't already rejected by a driver (that is, an unassigned route). Enter the reason for the callout.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select drivers" icon="users" titleType="h3">
    Choose which drivers to recruit. Only drivers eligible for the route are shown.

    <Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
      Work in progress: this page will also show each driver's current-week shift hours and rank drivers from lowest to highest hours.
    </Callout>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Edit the text message" icon="message-square" titleType="h3">
    Review the text template that will be sent to selected drivers. You can modify it — for example, to add an incentive for picking up the shift (extra pay, etc.).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review responses" icon="list-checks" titleType="h3">
    See how drivers have responded to the recruitment text. Once you have acceptances, select one of the drivers who replied yes to assign them the route.

    You can also navigate back to the **Select drivers** page to recruit more drivers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Text message behaviors

### Driver responses

Once a recruitment text goes out, a driver can reply:

- **Y** — to say they're available for the shift

- **N** — to decline

If a driver replies **Y**, they receive:

> Received, [Name]. You're on the list for the [Route Type and Time] shift on [Date]. We'll confirm shortly.

If a driver replies **N**, they receive:

> You have opted out of the [Route Type and Time] shift on [Date].

### Management notifications

When a driver accepts, every management contact on file receives a text:

> [Route Block ID] [Driver Name] opted into the shift recruitment for [Route Type and Time] shift on [Date].

### Confirming a driver

Once you select a driver to assign the route to:

- The selected driver receives a final confirmation text:

  > Confirmed, [Driver Name]: [Route Type and Time] shift on [Route Date]. Check Relay for full details — may take a few minutes to update.

- Every other driver who responded receives:

  > The [Route Type and Time] shift on [Route Date] has been filled by another driver. We'll notify you of the next available pickup.

### Canceling the recruitment

If AFP management cancels the recruitment flow, every driver who was originally texted receives:

> The [Route Type and Time] shift on [Route Date] is no longer available. We'll notify you of the next available pickup.
