---
title: Shift Patterns
description: >-
  Configure a 2 on 2 off or 4 on 4 off rotation for SO2 contract routes so
  Copilot assigns drivers automatically each week.
---

Shift Patterns let you define a recurring driver rotation — **2 on 2 off** or **4 on 4 off** — for Solo 2 (SO2) contract routes. Once a contract has a pattern configured, Dynamic Scheduler assigns its two drivers automatically according to the rotation, instead of requiring manual assignment each week.

This feature expands what was previously the **"4 on 4 off"** setting: the same toggle and workflow now also support a 2 on 2 off rotation.

---

## Enabling Shift Patterns for Your Workspace

SO2-only carriers should automatically see the ability to set Shift Patterns for their SO2 contracts. If you don't see this, or you'd like it enabled for your carrier, connect with our Support Team.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  [Submit a support ticket](/submit-a-support-ticket) to get Shift Patterns enabled for your workspace.
</Callout>

---

## Configuring a Shift Pattern on a Contract

1. Go to the **[Contracts tab](https://app.laminarcopilot.com/contracts)**.
2. Open the edit drawer for the SO2 contract you want to configure.
3. Set **Shift Pattern** to one of:
   - **None** — no rotation; the contract is scheduled as usual with no drivers pre-assigned to a pattern.
   - **2 on 2 off**
   - **4 on 4 off**

New contracts default to **None**. Selecting either rotation reveals two driver fields — assign the two drivers who will rotate on and off this contract's routes.

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  Shift Pattern can only be set on **SO2 (Solo 2)** contracts. SO1 contracts don't support this feature.
</Callout>

In the Contracts table, the old **4 on 4 off** column is now labeled **Shift Pattern** and shows the selected option (**None**, **2 on 2 off**, or **4 on 4 off**) instead of a Yes/No value.
