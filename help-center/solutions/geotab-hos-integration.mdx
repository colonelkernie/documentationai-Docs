GeoTab HOS Integration automatically pulls the actual start and end times of historic routes from GeoTab into Laminar Copilot. Once connected, these actual times populate on their own — no manual action required.

## Setting up GeoTab HOS Integration

GeoTab HOS Integration requires a one-time setup with our Support team to connect your GeoTab account.

<Callout kind="info">
  [Submit a support ticket](/submit-a-support-ticket) to get started. Our team will walk you through connecting your GeoTab account.
</Callout>

## How drivers are matched

GeoTab HOS Integration matches drivers between Copilot and GeoTab using their **phone number or email address**. For a route's actual times to sync to the correct driver, at least one of these must match exactly between the driver's record in Copilot's Driver Roster and their profile in GeoTab.

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  If neither a driver's phone number nor email matches between Copilot and GeoTab, their historic route times won't sync. If actual times aren't appearing as expected for a driver, check that their phone number or email matches on both sides first.
</Callout>

## How it works

Once your GeoTab account is connected, Copilot automatically pulls actual start and end times for historic routes from GeoTab — you don't need to do anything to trigger it.

This is the automatic counterpart to [Actual Route Start and End Times](/help-center/solutions/how-to-edit-actual-route-start-and-end-times): where Advanced Route Time Editing lets you manually correct a route's actual times, GeoTab HOS Integration fills them in for you automatically once a route is historic.
