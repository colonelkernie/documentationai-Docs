GeoTab HOS Integration automatically pulls the actual start and end times of historic routes from GeoTab into Laminar Copilot. Once connected, these actual times populate on their own — no manual action required.

## Setting up GeoTab HOS Integration

GeoTab HOS Integration requires a one-time setup with our Support team to connect your GeoTab account.

<Callout kind="info">
  [Submit a support ticket](/submit-a-support-ticket) to get started. Our team will walk you through connecting your GeoTab account.
</Callout>

If your workspace isn't connected yet, you'll also see a **Use Actual Hours** badge near the **Start Date** column on the Schedule tab in Command Center. Clicking it explains what the integration does and lets you book a setup call with our team. The badge disappears once your GeoTab account is connected.

## How drivers are matched

GeoTab HOS Integration matches drivers between Copilot and GeoTab using their **phone number or email address**. For a route's actual times to sync to the correct driver, at least one of these must match exactly between the driver's record in Copilot's Driver Roster and their profile in GeoTab.

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  If neither a driver's phone number nor email matches between Copilot and GeoTab, their historic route times won't sync. If actual times aren't appearing as expected for a driver, check that their phone number or email matches on both sides first. Drivers who don't match are flagged in the [Audit Panel](/help-center/solutions/the-audit-panel).
</Callout>

## How it works

Once your GeoTab account is connected, Copilot automatically pulls actual start and end times for historic routes from GeoTab — you don't need to do anything to trigger it.

This is the automatic counterpart to [Actual Route Start and End Times](/help-center/solutions/how-to-edit-actual-route-start-and-end-times): where Advanced Route Time Editing lets you manually correct a route's actual times, GeoTab HOS Integration fills them in for you automatically once a route is historic.

## When a route runs long

When GeoTab shows a route ending late enough that its driver won't have a full 10-hour reset before their next scheduled route, Copilot flags that next route instead of changing its schedule:

- The next route keeps its scheduled start time.
- The driver's assignment on that route is invalidated, because they're no longer eligible to run it. The route stays assigned to them until you reassign it.
- Copilot texts the driver and your AFP management contacts. See [Text Message Notifications](/help-center/solutions/text-message-notifications) for the wording.

To clear the flag, edit the route and assign a different driver, or unassign the driver.

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  Copilot only checks the driver's very next route. Routes further out in their schedule aren't flagged, and Copilot doesn't reassign the route for you.
</Callout>

If a route still leaves its driver a full 10-hour reset, nothing changes and no texts are sent.
