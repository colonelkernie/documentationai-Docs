# Import Routes into Copilot

> Uploading the CSV export from Relay, then choose to either our Dynamic Scheduler or a manual import.

**Source:** https://help.laminarcopilot.com/en/articles/12223341-import-routes-into-copilot  
**Collection:** Solutions > Managing Routes  
**Last Updated:** September 9, 2025

---

You can import routes into Laminar Copilot using a **CSV upload**. The file you upload must be the **CSV export of routes from Amazon Relay, found in the Trips tab**. After uploading, you can choose one of two scheduling methods depending on whether you want Copilot to auto-assign or keep assignments manual.

## Importing Routes via CSV Upload

1. From the Copilot dashboard, go to **Command Center** in the side navigation.
2. Click **Import Routes**.
3. Upload the CSV file that you've exported from Relay.
4. Select your scheduling method by clicking **Run Dynamic Scheduler** or **Import Routes**.

---

## Scheduling Methods

### 1. Dynamic Scheduler

- Copilot's scheduling algorithm automatically assigns routes to drivers and assets.
- Assignments are optimized based on:
  - Driver preferences
  - Driver availability
  - Asset availability
- After Dynamic Scheduler runs, you can review and make adjustments to assignments if needed.

---

### 2. Import Routes (Manual Assignments)

- Imports routes into your schedule without assigning drivers or assets.
- Useful if you want full control over assignments.
- All routes must be manually assigned to drivers and assets after import.

---

## Choosing the Right Method

- **Dynamic Scheduler:** Best if you want Copilot to automatically generate optimized assignments.
- **Import Routes (Manual):** Best if you prefer to assign drivers and assets yourself.
