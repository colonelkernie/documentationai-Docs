## Prerequisites

Before you begin, ensure you have the following:

<Callout kind="info">

- A modern web browser such as Chrome, Firefox, or Safari
- A valid email address for account verification
- Access to your email inbox for confirmation links

</Callout>

Laminar Copilot runs entirely in the browser at `https://app.laminarcopilot.com`, so no downloads or installations are required.

## Create and Verify Your Account

Sign up for Laminar Copilot in under a minute.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit the App" icon="globe">
    Open your browser and navigate to [`https://app.laminarcopilot.com`](https://app.laminarcopilot.com).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your Details" icon="user-plus">
    Click **Sign Up** and provide your email address and a secure password. You can also sign up using Google or GitHub for faster access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Email" icon="mail">
    Check your inbox for a verification email from Laminar Copilot. Click the link to confirm your account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  If you do not receive the verification email, check your spam folder or request a new one from the sign-up page.
</Callout>

## Log In to the Web App

Once verified, log in to access your dashboard.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Email & Password" icon="mail">
    Enter your email and password on the login page. Use the **Forgot Password** link if needed.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Social Login" icon="share-2">
    Click **Continue with Google** or **Continue with GitHub** for seamless authentication.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

After login, you land on the main dashboard.

## Set Up Your First Workspace

Workspaces organize your routes and audits. Create one to get started.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Workspace" icon="plus">
    From the dashboard, click **New Workspace**. Name it something like `My First Project`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Basics" icon="settings">
    Add a description and select your default region (e.g., US East). Save the workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule First Route" icon="calendar">
    Inside the workspace, click **New Route**. Set a simple audit path and schedule it to run immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Here is an example route configuration:

<CodeGroup tabs="JSON,CLI">
```json
{
  "name": "Basic Audit Route",
  "path": "/api/health",
  "frequency": "daily",
  "regions": ["us-east-1"]
}
```
```bash
laminar route create --name "Basic Audit" --path "/api/health" --freq daily
```
</CodeGroup>
